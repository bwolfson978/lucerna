# Walkthrough video script

For ChatGPT's review (`demo_recording_url`). Record in ChatGPT with the plugin connected, upload as an unlisted YouTube or Loom video, and put the link in `listing.json` as `demoRecordingUrl`. Aim for 2 to 3 minutes. Sign out of the reviewer account before recording, so the sign-in step shows.

Talking is optional; the captions below can be said or typed as on-screen notes.

| Time | Do | Say or show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Show a new chat with Roth Conversions Optimizer enabled. | "Roth Conversions Optimizer works out how much a Roth conversion could save in tax over a lifetime. No account needed." |
| 0:10 | Type: *I'm 55, married, earn $90K, and have $800K in a traditional IRA. We retire at 62 in Texas. Could Roth conversions help?* | The scenario card appears with the values filled in. |
| 0:30 | Point at the card, then press **Run the numbers**. | "Nothing runs until you confirm the numbers." |
| 0:40 | Scroll the result card: savings, the bracket chart, the convert and tax rows. | "Estimated lifetime tax savings in today's dollars, and how each year fills the brackets." |
| 1:05 | Type: *What if we retire at 58 instead?* | The what-if card compares both savings figures. |
| 1:25 | Press **See full results** on a card and show the website opening. | "Every result opens as a full interactive analysis." |
| 1:40 | Back in the chat, type: *Save this scenario as Retire at 62 in Texas.* | The sign-in prompt appears. |
| 1:50 | Sign in as reviewer@rothconversionsoptimizer.com. | "Signing in is only needed to save." |
| 2:10 | Type: *Show my saved scenarios.* Then: *Open the first one.* | The list shows the new scenario and the three samples; opening one brings back its values. |
| 2:35 | End on the result card. | "Educational scenario analysis, not financial advice." |
